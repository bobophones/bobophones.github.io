Wooden Modular Outdoor Set
This modern, garden sofa set allows you to tailor furniture to your own outdoor space. The low platform seating with padded cushions offer a calming lounging experience. The modular design allows customisation to your space, including the corner, a coffee table and extra room either side of the seating. Enjoy summer nights with an intimate group of friends, or stretch out and read a book alone. Longevity is assured, as it is made from robust aluminium and certified Acacia wood.
